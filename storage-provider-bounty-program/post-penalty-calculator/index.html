⚠️ Important Notes

• The fault fee multiplier in Filecoin's protocol is 1.5× the expected daily block reward per sector.

• After 2 consecutive days of faults: the sector is terminated (provably expired). Termination penalty is approximately 20 days of expected block reward.

• These are estimates. Actual penalties depend on network base fees, gas costs for submitting recovery proofs, and current block reward levels.

• You can reduce or eliminate the penalty by recovering your faulty sectors (submitting valid WindowPoST proofs) before the end of the proving period.
